# Limits

- Public document only; no access to underlying data, code or grader outputs.
- The review cannot verify that any reported number is true.
- Figures were read from image captures of the PDF; some sample sizes are derived (positives divided by rates) and marked as such.
- One reviewer; ratings are judgment calls. A second independent rating is planned but not done.
- Consequence ratings (Low / Medium / High) are indicative, not calibrated.
- Scope is four sections of one card. Nothing is said about the rest of the card, about capability or safeguards evaluations, or about the model.
- Reporting quality in a public document is not the same as the quality of the underlying work.
- The review may be out of date if the card has changed since the access date.
- Nothing here is legal advice or a statement about compliance with any regulation.
